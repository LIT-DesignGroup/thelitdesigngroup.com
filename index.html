Who We Are

About LIT

Our Story

Lighting Specialists
You Can Trust

LIT Design Group was founded with a simple belief: great lighting transforms how you experience your home and property. What started as a holiday lighting operation has grown into a full-service outdoor lighting company serving clients across the DC Metro area.

Our team brings deep expertise across all three pillars of outdoor illumination — landscape and architectural uplighting, permanent programmable systems, and seasonal holiday displays. We work with premium products and proven installation techniques to deliver results that last.

As the parent company of HoLITday Designs, we've built our reputation on meticulous craftsmanship, exceptional client service, and a genuine passion for the art of light.

Design-Forward

Every project starts with a plan. We design before we install — ensuring the final result is intentional and beautiful.

Reliable & Responsive

We show up on time, communicate clearly, and stand behind our work with best-in-class warranties and support.

Premium Products

We partner with industry-leading manufacturers to ensure every component is built to perform and built to last.

What We Offer

Our Services

Year-Round Service

Landscape & Uplighting

Professional low-voltage landscape lighting that enhances your property's architecture, highlights natural features, and extends the usability of your outdoor spaces after dark.

We design, install, and maintain complete systems — from pathway lighting and garden spots to dramatic tree and facade uplighting.

  • Architectural & facade uplighting
  • Garden & pathway lighting
  • Tree & hardscape illumination
  • Smart control integration
  • Lifetime warranty products available
Always On. Always Ready.

Permanent Lighting

Install once, celebrate forever. Permanent programmable LED systems are built into your roofline and eaves, completely invisible during the day and spectacular at night.

Control colors, patterns, and schedules from your phone — perfect for every holiday, event, or every day.

  • Concealed roofline installation
  • 16 million+ color combinations
  • Smartphone app control
  • Weather-resistant commercial grade
  • No ladders, no ladders, no ladders
